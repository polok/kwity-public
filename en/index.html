Everything that loses value, under control

Kwity keeps track of the end of a warranty, a return window, a bottle deposit, a parking ticket and a queue number. Snap a receipt and we remind you before it is too late.

Download on the App Store Support Privacy policy

Receipt scanning

The document camera or a photo from your library. Store, amount and date are read on your phone.

Warranties and returns

Day countdowns, categories and a list of what ends soon.

For a moment

Deposits, parking and queue tickets with a live countdown. They stay until you clear them.

QR codes and barcodes

Show a ticket's code with one tap. The screen brightens by itself and the code is drawn sharply.

Reminders

A notification before a deadline, set separately for every receipt.

iCloud sync (Pro)

Optional, a one-time purchase: your receipts and photos on all your devices, in your own private iCloud.

Private

No account, no ads. Your receipts and photos stay on your phone or in your own iCloud. Face ID lock, dark and light looks.
