Frequently asked questions

How do I add a receipt?

On the bottom bar tap “Scan receipt” to photograph a receipt, or the plus to add one by hand. The app reads the store, amount and date, and you check the form and save. The product name is always marked “check”, because it is the easiest thing to miss.

I have no camera, or I prefer a photo from the library

The scanning screen has a “From library” button. You can also pick a photo from your library when adding a receipt by hand.

The receipt was not read well

Take the photo flat, in good light, with the text in focus. Recognition can make mistakes, so always check the data before saving. Every field can be corrected by hand.

What is the difference between a warranty and a return window?

Both have an end date and go into a category. A warranty is usually months or years, a return a few or a dozen days. After the date, or once marked as used, the receipt goes to the archive in the “More” menu.

What is “For a moment” and why does a ticket not disappear on its own?

These are deposits, parking and queue tickets with a countdown. When time runs out they land in “Expired” and stay there until you remove them, because you may not have reached the meter or the bottle machine yet.

How do I show a ticket's code?

If a receipt has a QR code or barcode, it has a code button on the home screen and “Show code to scan” in the details. The code is drawn sharply on white and the screen brightens to the maximum. You can scan the code with the camera in the form or type it in.

I am not getting reminders

In the “More” menu check that the “Reminders” switch is on. If iOS blocks notifications, turn them on in your phone's Settings. A reminder will not fire if its moment has already passed, and the app warns you about that when you save.

How do I lock the app with Face ID?

In the “More” menu turn on “Security”. The app locks when it goes to the background and at launch. If Face ID fails, you can unlock it with your device passcode.

Where is my data, and does it survive a new phone?

By default your receipts and photos are only on the device, with no account and no app cloud (anonymous statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase). If you buy iCloud sync, they are also copied to your own private iCloud and appear on your other devices. Without it, moving to a new phone brings your data along with an iCloud or computer backup, if you have one. Deleting the app deletes the data from the phone.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. The app is free. The menu has two voluntary purchases: “Buy me a coffee” (a tip for the developer, it unlocks nothing) and “Pro features”, which is iCloud sync (one time, for good, no subscription).

How does iCloud sync work?

In the “More” menu go to “Pro features” → “iCloud sync”, buy the feature, then close Kwity and open it again. Your receipts and photos appear on your other devices with the same Apple ID and iCloud turned on, sometimes after a few minutes. On a new phone choose “Restore purchases”. You can switch sync off with the “Sync” switch.
